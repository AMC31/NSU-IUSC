Design and build a premium, immersive, highly responsive tournament website for **NSU Inter University Sports Carnival (NSUSC) — Season 3, 2026**, a flagship inter-university sports tournament where universities from across Bangladesh participate and compete for the title of the best university in sports.

The website should feel like a combination of a **professional international sports championship website, modern university event platform, and high-energy digital tournament hub**.

The final product must look production-ready, polished, trustworthy, exciting, and premium. It must be designed for both desktop and mobile from the beginning rather than treating mobile as an afterthought.

## 1. Core Brand & Experience

Event name:
**NSU INTER UNIVERSITY SPORTS CARNIVAL**
**SEASON 3 — 2026**

Primary purpose of the website:

* Easy tournament registration
* University/team registration
* Tournament information
* Sports/event details
* Match schedules
* Fixtures
* Results
* Live tournament updates
* Tournament standings
* Prize pool information
* Rules and regulations
* Venues
* Important announcements
* FAQs
* Contact/support
* Direct communication with NSUSC Club members through a help chat system

The overall experience should communicate:

**Competition + Prestige + Energy + University Spirit + Technology + Trust**

The website should immediately make visitors feel:

“Something major is happening here.”

Avoid making it look like a generic university website or a simple event landing page.

## 25. Responsive Design

This requirement is extremely important.

Design the complete experience responsively for:

* Desktop: 1440px+
* Laptop: 1280px
* Tablet: 768px
* Mobile: 390px / 375px

Do not simply shrink desktop designs.

Adapt layouts intelligently.

Desktop:

* Multi-column layouts
* Large hero
* Side-by-side content
* Data tables

Tablet:

* Reduced columns
* Rebalanced spacing
* Responsive cards

Mobile:

* Single-column layouts
* Horizontal scrolling cards where useful
* Bottom-sheet filters
* Compact tables converted into cards
* Sticky mobile CTA
* Large readable text
* Large touch targets
* No horizontal page overflow

Ensure:

* Minimum 44px touch targets
* Comfortable spacing
* Readable typography
* Accessible contrast
